The last two nights: move down to Jibhi

You check out of Suhaana Ghar on the 2nd, and you drive home on the 4th, so you need somewhere for two more nights. I’d move to Jibhi in the Tirthan valley, about 2 hours from Shangarh (via Sainj, Larji and Banjar). It has a completely different feel from Shangarh: rivers, deodar forest and Jalori Pass. It also takes about an hour off the drive home. Friday 2 Oct is Gandhi Jayanti, so it’s a long weekend and Jibhi fills up. Book this week.

Want to stay put instead? Ask Suhaana Ghar about extending two nights and do Jibhi and Jalori as a long day trip on the 3rd. That’s about 2.5 hours each way, so it’s the tiring option.

Thu 1 Oct

Slow Shangarh day

  1. 06:15
    Sunrise at the meadow and temple

    The quietest hour. Dew on the grass, first light on the wooden tiers of the temple, and snow peaks to the east. Visitors are welcome at the temple, but be respectful and don’t take photos of any ceremonies.

    Map
  2. 09:30
    Barshangarh waterfall

    About 3.5 km from the village. Drive most of the way on the forest road, then it’s an easy 10–15 minute walk over log bridges to a 30 m fall. Allow about 2 hours in all. The rocks are slippery, so wear shoes with grip.

    Map
  3. 13:00
    Lunch at Pablo’s

    Pahadi rajma or kadhi chawal on the terrace. Be patient: everything is cooked fresh.

    Map
  4. Afternoon
    A village walk, or nothing at all

    Wander the lanes past kath-kuni houses and apple orchards, or do the first stretch of the Lapah trail through the forest. This is the slow afternoon to read on the veranda.

  5. Evening
    Bonfire and an early night

    Ask your hosts about siddu for dinner. It’s a steamed local bread with a walnut or poppy-seed filling.

    Map

Jalori Pass day

Why not Murthal?

At 05:00 you'll pass Murthal around 06:15. That's too early for a real coffee stop and too soon to need one, so save your appetite for Kurukshetra.

After Aut

The Sainj–Shangarh stretch is single-lane in places. Try to be past Sainj before dark, and fill up at Aut: fuel pumps get scarce after that.

Post-monsoon check

It's the end of September, so check HP road status for the Mandi–Aut and Sainj stretches the evening before you leave.
